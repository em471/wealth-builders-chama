Interest = Loan Repaid − previous month's Loan Taken. Check = Loan Repaid − previous Loan Taken × (1 + rate): OK when exact, red when short, amber when over. Checks stay blank until a month has any savings entered.

Collected vs disbursed

Each month's pool = undisbursed brought forward + savings + principal repaid + interest collected. The pool less loans disbursed is what carries into the next month. Lending began in March, so the January and February savings form part of the March pool.
